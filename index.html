Journal · No. 01

From the desk

April 2026
Essay Six minute read Updated 26 April 2026

The studio is small on purpose. One desk, one lamp, a stack of paper that refuses to be tidy. This is a short note about why it stays that way — and what is being made inside it this season.

For a long time the work lived in private notebooks, the kind that travel between bag and table and rarely get opened in front of anyone. This site is the first attempt to set some of that work down in public. It collects fifteen pieces from the recent ink series, twelve certifications earned along the way, and a small set of Hindi lessons prepared for adult beginners.

What is on the site, and where to find it

Two pages do most of the heavy lifting. The Arts page is a column gallery of the fifteen original works — drawings and ink pieces shown at full bleed, in the order they were made. The Certifications page collects the twelve accredited certificates that sit behind the teaching practice, scanned at their original size and arranged chronologically.

Both can be reached from the menu at the top of every page. The homepage, where this article sits, is meant as a quiet dashboard — a single screen that gives the count of works, points to the two galleries, and lets a visitor decide which door to open first.

What changed in this update

Three things, all small. The Arts and Certifications collections each moved into their own dedicated page, so that one no longer crowds the other. The homepage was rebuilt as a proper studio dashboard, with a statistics strip and a card grid that links into the rest of the site. And this article — the one in front of you — was rewritten from the original placeholder so that it actually says something about the practice.

What comes next

A sixteenth artwork is on the desk now, in the early stages where nothing is fixed and everything still might be wrong. After that, a printed edition of the Hindi primer, and — if the year allows it — a modest exhibition of the ink series, hung in the order the pieces were first drawn.

Until then, the gallery is open. Thank you for visiting the studio.
